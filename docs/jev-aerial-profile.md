`sh_nair`. Both earlier profiles keep their existing ordered labels. The new
choice uses the existing Fox main-ground short-hop Nair primitive, its settled
ground/fresh-input/geometry guards, apply-time validation and observed commitment.

and asynchronous selectors receive the same legal catalog.

reduced landing lag. Raw policy evidence reports short hop, native aerial
acknowledgement, completion, L-cancel attempts and measured landing-duration
counts separately. It never infers a successful L-cancel from the button press.
Interrupted aerials retain their failure. The local defensive reflex can still
send its own tech pulse after cancelling the aerial; that is a separate owner.

selection. The corpus compiler now also hashes the aerial implementation. A
new-profile corpus needs its own compatible source sessions; old recordings
and compiler identities are preserved. No fine-tuning is introduced.
